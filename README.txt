BAYANI BITE - Campus Rescue Board

OPEN: Double-click dist/index.html in a modern browser. No install, login,
internet connection, database or API is required. JavaScript must be enabled.

VISUAL BRAND: Blue-and-cream dashboard with the supplied BAYANI BITES mascot
in the sidebar and Descriptive hero. The original image is embedded in the HTML
for standalone offline use and also included in dist/assets/ for reuse.

1. DESCRIPTIVE: Move the week slider and change the chart metric to explore
   observed Weeks 1-12 KPIs and trends. Open the KPI definitions for inputs.
2. DIAGNOSTIC: Inspect cost/revenue clues, the rival explanation and survey.
   Use Whole day / Peak / Nonpeak for the Week 12 branch snapshot.
3. PRESCRIPTIVE: Select A-E or use the package buttons. Costs, lower-bound
   planning impacts, failure impact, ongoing profit and budget feasibility update.
4. Open the KPI definitions and Trust & sources notes for calculation evidence.
   PREDICTIVE shows the time split: Weeks 1-11 history (11 weeks), Week 12
   validation holdout (1 week), and Week 13 onward future testing pending actuals.
   The four tabs preserve control settings while switching views. Keyboard:
   use Left/Right or Home/End when a tab is focused. Legacy #pilot and #forecast
   links open Prescriptive and Predictive respectively.
5. Bulletin 01 is applied by default: budget 27k; C weekly uplift 1k-4k,
   with its cost unchanged at 8k. The final recommendation is A alone (18k
   setup, +6k four-week impact), compared with feasible A+C (26k, -5k).
   Open the bulletin controls to read the source or explore assumptions.
   Reload supplied Bulletin 01 restores the official updated scenario.
   Download the decision record to retain any manual session edits.

INITIAL RECORD: A+C originally cost 26k and gave +7k four-week impact. Under
the actual bulletin it still costs 26k, but now gives -5k: a 12k deterioration.
The intermediate pre-bulletin C recommendation is retained in the AI audit.
The measurable objective is positive cumulative incremental operating profit
after setup (>0) versus doing nothing by Week 16, within the 27k budget and
with cash access, existing budget meals and staff consent protected. A is the
highest-benefit feasible planning package at +6k. Actual outcomes await the pilot.
Zero-uplift failure for A is -18k; ongoing weekly profit is -28.7k.

The one-file HTML embeds all five original CSV tables. Historical observations
remain fixed. The original packet and CSVs are in source-data/; the three
authoritative bulletin files are in source-data/bulletin01/ in the ZIP.
verification.json and prepare.py document independent deterministic checks.
dashboard-screenshot.png is a captured view of the working dashboard.
pilot-screenshot.png shows the updated decision; forecast-screenshot.png shows
the unchanged history/holdout/future-testing split.

Data: fictional teaching case, student packet revised 20 October 2026.
